# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This policy explains how Anitabi App handles information related to use of the app.

## 1. How we handle data

The app has no accounts or advertising and does not sell user information. Favorites, pilgrimage plans, settings, map caches, and captured or generated comparison images are stored on your device by default and are not uploaded to servers operated by this project.

When you choose to share or import a pilgrimage plan, the plan name and spot identifiers are written into a QR code, share link, or plan file. They are transmitted only through the system share destination you select. This project does not operate a relay server.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may delete this content. Your device's system backup may store a separate copy.

## 2. Permissions

The app requests permissions only when you use the corresponding feature:

- **Location**: Shows your current position on the map.
- **Camera**: Captures pilgrimage comparison photos and scans pilgrimage-plan QR codes.
- **Photos and media library**: Selects reference images and saves generated images.

You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings, but the related features may become unavailable. This project does not actively upload your location or image content to its own servers.

## 3. Third-party services

The app connects to the following third-party services:

- **anitabi.cn**: Provides location, anime, and image data.
- **Mapbox**: Loads the map.
- **Sentry**: Receives technical diagnostics such as crashes, performance data, app version, and device type. Default transmission of personally identifiable information (PII) is disabled.

These services may process necessary network information such as IP addresses, request times, and device types according to their own privacy policies.

## 4. Policy changes and contact

We will update this file if the app's data handling changes. For privacy questions, contact the maintainers through an Issue in this repository.
