# Designed for every step of an anime pilgrimage

Anitabi puts anime location data on a mobile map and builds browsing, favorites, planning, and photography around the needs of a real journey.

## Map exploration

- Browse anime pilgrimage spots around the world.
- Filter the map by anime title to reduce unrelated results.
- View the distribution of pilgrimage spots and their related images.
- Open a spot's details and launch navigation directly in a map app.

## Search and favorites

- Search for anime titles and pilgrimage spots.
- Save places you want to visit or have already visited.
- Favorites are stored locally, with no account required.

## Pilgrimage plans

Organize multiple spots into a dedicated plan and view them together on the map. It is useful for arranging a day's route before departure and quickly switching destinations while traveling.

## Comparison photography

Open the camera on site and use an anime screenshot as a composition guide. After shooting, you can replace the live photo, generate a comparison image, and save it to your photo library.

## Dark mode and local-first storage

The app supports the system dark mode. Favorites, plans, cached data, and comparison images are stored on your device by default. Location, camera, and photo permissions are used only when their corresponding features need them.

::: tip Actively evolving
Pilgrimage plans can be shared as images with QR codes, while larger routes can be imported from plan files. AI-assisted route planning and other capabilities are planned. Suggestions are welcome through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/boiboif/anitabi-app/issues).
:::
